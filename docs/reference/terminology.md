# Terminology

- Issue date: when an NFS-e was issued.
- Service competence: when the service belongs economically/legally.
- Cash receipt date: when revenue was actually received.
- PGDAS PA: assessment period selected in PGDAS-D.
- Payroll competence: month payroll belongs to.
- Payroll payment date: when the person was paid.
- eSocial period: period reported in eSocial events.
- DCTFWeb period: period of the federal declaration.
- Due date: legal deadline to pay/submit.
- Payment date: when payment actually occurred.
- Original declaration: first transmitted declaration for a period.
- Retificadora: later correction; retain both.
