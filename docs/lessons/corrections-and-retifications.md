# Corrections and Retifications

Never overwrite a prior filing record after a retificadora.

Keep:

- original filing,
- correction filing,
- reason for correction,
- source evidence,
- tax difference,
- payment/refund/offset consequence,
- date of transmission.

## Correct in chronological order

If a later PGDAS period exposes an earlier historical error through `RBA`, `RBT12`, `RBT12p`, Factor R inputs, or other cumulative values, stop the later filing and correct the earlier period first.

After the earlier retificadora is transmitted, reopen/recalculate the later period so PGDAS can rebuild its cumulative history from the corrected data.

## A retificadora does not automatically mean another payment

A correction can alter historical competence information while leaving the cash-basis taxable revenue and total debt unchanged.

When this happens:

1. preserve the original declaration and DAS,
2. preserve the retificadora,
3. check `Consultar Declarações` / the DAS extract for the original payment status,
4. do not generate or pay a duplicate merely because the newer declaration exists,
5. only pay a new DAS when PGDAS establishes an actual remaining difference.

Record explicitly whether the correction changed:

- competence revenue,
- cash revenue,
- activity segregation,
- payroll/Factor R inputs,
- total declared debt,
- amount still payable.

## Zero-revenue corrections

If source evidence establishes that a period had no competence revenue and no cash receipts, a retificadora may legitimately set both to zero and result in zero tax. Retain the receipt proving the correction was transmitted.
