# Factor R

Factor R depends on payroll and revenue history over the relevant lookback period. Do not calculate it from one payroll event in isolation.

Any Factor R calculation must state:

- included revenue periods,
- included payroll/pro-labore payments,
- evidence IDs,
- legal source and last verification date.

## Current durable rule

Under Resolução CGSN nº 140/2018, art. 26, Factor R is the ratio between payroll including charges in the relevant prior-12-month period and gross revenue in the corresponding prior-12-month period.

For qualifying services:

- Factor R >= 0,28: Annex III.
- Factor R < 0,28: Annex V.

Dentistry is among the activities subject to Factor R.

For companies with less than 13 months of activity, use the regulation's special proportionalization rules rather than treating the business as if it already had a full twelve-month history.

## Payment timing matters

The regulation describes the payroll component as amounts **paid** in the 12 months before the PA, including remuneration/pro-labore and applicable charges. Therefore:

- payroll competence,
- eSocial payment event month,
- actual bank payment date,
- Factor R inclusion month

must not be collapsed into one date.

If remuneration for one competence is paid in the following month, model Factor R using the actual payment timing required by the regulation and the evidence in private state.

## Optimization rule

Do not optimize Factor R in isolation. Before increasing pró-labore, compare:

- expected Simples tax under Annex III versus Annex V,
- additional INSS/payroll cost,
- company liquidity,
- personal IR consequences,
- whether the remuneration remains commercially and legally defensible.

A higher legitimate pró-labore can improve future Factor R, but the goal is not to create artificial payroll solely to cross 0,28.
